Questions

Answers before you ask.

What exactly is a 3PL?

A third-party logistics provider — companies like ShipBob and ShipMonk that store your inventory, pick & pack orders, and ship them for you. DTC brands outsource to a 3PL when in-house fulfillment starts eating too much of the founder's week. That transition is when they sign up for a new provider — the exact moment your affiliate link earns commission.

Do I need affiliate accounts before signing up?

No — Miri ships with 5 pre-configured programs (ShipBob, ShipMonk, ShipHero, Easyship, Red Stag). You can start pitching immediately using generic links, then swap them for your own affiliate URLs once you're accepted into each program. Applications typically clear in 24-72 hours.

Where do the leads come from?

Public web search plus a Shopify-signal detector — no scraped databases, no purchased lists. Every lead is a real, public DTC store surfaced from a live search. Miri only stores what you'd see in a browser: brand name, URL, contact email if public, and computed fit signals.

Is this GDPR / CAN-SPAM compliant?

Miri drafts emails; it doesn't send them. You review every message and hit send from your own mail client. That keeps you in the driver's seat on CAN-SPAM (physical address, opt-out link, honest subject line) and GDPR (legitimate-interest basis + easy opt-out on first reply).

Can I bring my own partner programs?

Yes. On the Pro and Scale plans you can add unlimited partners with your own commission %, vertical specialty, volume range, and referral URL template. The matcher scores every partner on every lead so your best-paying program isn't buried under generalists.
